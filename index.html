How a project works

Care, craft and collaboration.

1. Introductory meeting

Jon meets you, preferably at the project site, to learn your vision.

2. Budget and schedule

We set a preliminary budget range and a forecast of how long the project will take.

3. Preconstruction

Permitting, scope of work and a pre-construction meeting before work begins.

4. Construction

Weekly meetings and bi-weekly status reports until your home is done.
